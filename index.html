There once lived a girl who believed fairy tales were real.

“Behind every Good Taste is a Great Story that paved its way to it!”

Little did she know that she would manifest the beautiful house she had heard about in one such story into reality through Lady Loafella.

A hide-out away from the urban life, a garden yard filled with the chirpings of birds and waves of laughter of kids and grown-ups alike.

Meet the lady behind Lady Loafella

Lady Loafella is a woman-owned enterprise and a reflection of strong dedication and passion for baking, without compromising an inch on quality and integrity.

Each bake from our oven is a product of love and labour, handcrafted and served with the smallest details taken care of. From the quality of the ingredients to the aesthetics of the product, every item on the menu will take you to a different part of the globe.

2011

From interior design to loaves

She poured her creative spirit from interior designing into the world of loaves and bakes.

3 years

An expedition of discovery

Research into loaves from across the globe, plus relentless training and tasting, until she could set the chef’s hat straight and bring the best breads to her hometown. She knew she was creating a new culture and an experience that few had access to until then.

Ella

The name behind the name

She was brought into the world of baking by one of her best friends. Ella represents Lady Loafella and her journey to bring world classics into the heart of Malabar.

What we are building, one loaf at a time

Our Mission

To continue to be the distinctive Royal Bread Boutique Experience that we are. To be nothing like the rest and still be the best of all, with incomparable quality of products and services.

Our Goal

To pave the way to a never-before bread boutique experience in the region. To create a feeling of travelling through the streets of Europe with authentic taste and flavour through our fresh bakes.

Our Vision

To be the grinding force that binds and inspires bakers to turn their baker dreams into reality. To be the facade of women entrepreneurship for passionate lady bakers from across the globe.
